# Imperial Essence Trading LLC

Official B2B luxury wholesale & distribution website for Imperial Essence Trading LLC, headquartered in Bay Square, Business Bay, Dubai, UAE.

## Company Overview
Imperial Essence Trading LLC draws on over 40 years of family legacy in luxury perfumery, niche fragrances, cosmetics, skincare, fashion, textiles, travel sets, accessories, and fine jewelry with direct European T1 & T2 sourcing flexibility.

## Tech Stack
- Next.js 16 (App Router)
- React 19 / TypeScript
- Vanilla CSS + Tailwind Utility Tokens
- Multi-language Support (English & Arabic)
